# LinkedIn Internship Tracker — UX Case Study

A student-focused redesign concept exploring how LinkedIn can better support first-year students through the internship application process.

---

## The Problem

First-year students don't struggle with *finding* internships as much as they struggle with *managing* them. Applications get lost, deadlines get missed, and LinkedIn's current interface doesn't give beginners much to hold onto. This project digs into that gap.

---

## Research

Conducted a survey among first and second-year engineering students to understand how they actually navigate internship applications — what tools they use, where they get stuck, and what they wish existed.

**Key findings:**
- Most students track applications through Notes, Notion, or spreadsheets — nothing inside LinkedIn
- First-year students frequently describe the interface as overwhelming
- Application status visibility is nearly nonexistent after submitting
- Internship recommendations often feel irrelevant to beginners
- Reminders and deadlines have no dedicated space

## User Persona

**Aarav Sharma** — 18, BTech CSE, 1st Year

> *"Applying is easy. Tracking everything is not."*

A student actively exploring internships but losing steam trying to stay organized across scattered tools. Motivated, slightly overwhelmed, and looking for a workflow that doesn't require five apps.

---

## Wireframes

Low-fidelity screens covering four core flows:
- Internship Dashboard (Kanban-style tracker)
- Beginner-friendly Discovery feed
- Application detail + reminders
- Resume organizer

## Proposed Direction

A student layer inside LinkedIn — not a new app, not a full redesign. Just a focused set of features that help beginners stay organized, track progress, and feel less lost during the application cycle.

---

## What's Next

High-fidelity UI, a clickable prototype, and usability testing with actual first-year students. This is a living project.
